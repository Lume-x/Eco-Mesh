OUR MISSION & ARCHITECTURE

Democratizing Connectivity with
Clean Solar Mesh Networks.

Legacy telecom giants neglect student hostels and peri-urban communities with congested cellular towers, frequent blackout collapses, and extractive 30-day data expiration countdowns. EcoMesh exists to replace diesel dependency with clean, decentralized community infrastructure.

THE STATUS QUO PROBLEM

Why Traditional Telecom Fails Off-Campus Students

Blackout Collapses & Diesel Scarcity When the municipal grid drops, cellular base stations rely on noisy diesel generators. When fuel runs out, entire campus hostels suffer total blackout connectivity drops during exam weeks.
Artificial Expiration & Stolen Megabytes Carriers systematically wipe unused data balances at the end of 30 days, forcing cash-strapped students to continually repurchase megabytes they already paid for.
Severe Evening Congestion Thousands of students competing for the same 4G/5G mast leads to packet loss exceeding 40% between 7 PM and 11 PM.
THE ECOMESH REVOLUTION

The Solar-Powered Mesh Solution

100% Off-Grid Solar Autonomy Each node is an independent micro-generator equipped with 400W Monocrystalline PV and 2.4kWh Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) storage, giving 72 hours of autonomy even in continuous rain.
Perpetual Non-Expiring Data Wallet Every megabyte purchased stays in your personal EcoMesh wallet forever. Rollover is unconditional and perpetual.
Dynamic Multi-Hop Routing Rooftop nodes communicate peer-to-peer over low-latency 5.8 GHz directional links, routing around physical obstacles automatically.

Hardware & Network Specifications

Open, modular, and field-repairable hardware designed for high-heat environments.

Solar Power Subsystem

400W Monocrystalline PV + Smart MPPT Charge Controller with 99.2% tracking efficiency and temperature compensation.

Dual-Band Radio

2x2 MIMO 5.8 GHz backhaul bridge (up to 900 Mbps) coupled with high-density 2.4 GHz 802.11ax client access arrays.

WPA3 Security

Individual 8-character hex PIN authentication via WPA3-Enterprise portal. Client isolation prevents cross-device snooping.

Live Telemetry SLA

Over-the-air MQTT telemetry reporting battery voltage, solar irradiation, client density, and link packet quality in real time.
